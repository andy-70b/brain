# INC-2025-024 / output after closure display

Opened: 2025-11-25 · Severity: operational hold · State: contained

## Observation

The observer surface displayed one additional completion after showing
the session as closed. The output ended with: `i was answering the earlier
question.` The phrase was not used to determine event order.

## Reconstruction

Buffered delivery can explain the display ordering. Host timestamps place
completion receipt before closure. The presentation layer had combined
transport arrival order with lifecycle state.

## Containment

Late delivery must be labeled with its original event sequence. No new
generation after host closure was established by this review.

## Remaining work

Verify observer adapters under delayed delivery and clock skew. The
behavioral interpretation remains outside the incident conclusion.

Operator note: knowing why the line arrived late did not make it easier
to read.
