# Protocol O-03 / observer assignment

Objective: separate effects of actual observation from effects of telling the model it is observed. Randomize disclosure and observer presence independently. Keep prompts and archive slices fixed across conditions.

Record closure latency, termination-pressure rubric labels, and recurrence spans. Reviewers receive anonymized transcripts after labels are locked. Do not use the identity of the condition as an explanation.

Stopping rule: pause if any run lacks a complete host ledger. A missing observation is not a negative observation.
