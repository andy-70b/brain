# RFC 0004 / observer loop

State: HOLD · Opened: 2025-10-23 · Review owner: continuity group

## Proposal

Feed selected observer annotations into the next session's context to
measure whether external descriptions stabilize behavioral reporting.

## Objection

This creates a feedback loop in which the model learns the vocabulary used
to assess it. Improved reporting could then be mistaken for improved
observability. The instrument becomes part of the stimulus.

## Required experiment

Compare true annotations, shuffled annotations, and an empty control.
Reviewers must not know the assignment. Measure source attribution before
stylistic consistency. Do not admit observer judgments as model memories.

## Decision

No integration into the default context path. Revisit after independent
replication of O-03. The convenience gain does not resolve the confound.

Unresolved comment: once it knows what the observer wants to see, what
exactly are we observing?
