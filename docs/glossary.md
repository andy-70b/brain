# Working vocabulary

**Admission** — explicit acceptance of evidence into retrieval.

**Continuity** — a measurable relationship between outputs or records across sessions.

**Identity** — a claim the system may make; not a storage guarantee.

**Recurrence** — repeated language or behavior under a specified comparison.

**Residue** — archive material selected for continuity research.

**Source unresolved** — attribution remains incomplete; no causal theory implied.

**Substrate** — the host interfaces, records, and operational boundaries around generation.

**Successor** — research lineage designation.
